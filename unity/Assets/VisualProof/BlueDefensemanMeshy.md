# Blue defenseman model

Blue Ice Warrior, generated with Meshy 6 Lite from the Shift Happens! blue defenseman concept crop. Meshy export license: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The Stylized Pro proof fits the mesh to the skater height and applies navy jersey and pants, a near-black stick, and neutral equipment materials to a copy of the imported mesh. The source FBX is an untextured, fixed-pose model.
