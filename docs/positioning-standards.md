# Shift Happens! positioning and decision standards

This standard applies to the Unity 12U practice. The project’s `docs/handoff-validation.md` records the approved Original and Marked behavior. The Behind net drills and their scoring remain draft. Outside references inform the teaching approach. Team-approved Box + 1 assignments govern the game.

## Standards for each read

- **Identify the threat.** Name the player, dangerous space or passing lane the skater owns. Describe the skater’s relationship to that threat and the net. The approved Marked hold can protect the feed from inside the passing lane.
- **Identify the cue.** Use a change the player can see in live play. A teammate arriving near a target, a carrier crossing a landmark and a teammate establishing coverage are distinct events.
- **Assign the handoff.** Identify who owns the threat before and after the cue. In the approved Original and Marked drills, RD must establish goal-side coverage, within reach and in the passing lane, face the play and settle before LD can contain from inside. The draft Behind net drill gives RD the far post and then the exiting carrier. LD owns Red C throughout that sequence.
- **Evaluate the read and the recovery.** Record the opening the decision gave Red, any recovery and the eventual puck outcome. A defender can recover in time to redirect a pass after conceding the initial look. A deflection leaves a loose puck until a player establishes control.
- **Teach through live decisions.** Show the three jobs around the net first. Add wingers and options once players can recognize pressure, support and net-front responsibility. Keep the cue consistent while changing puck side, opponent choice and timing.

## Behind net draft rule in Unity

| Phase | LD’s job | What the game checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approach and crossing | Stay goal-side and within reach of Red C while RD guards the far post. | **Hold inside** evaluates LD’s coverage before Red exits. |
| Far-post exit | Stay with Red C as RD takes the carrier. | **Cover Red C** evaluates the coverage window after Red exits. A continuous half-second of open slot lane during the carry triggers Red’s slot look and marks this read Missed. The half-second is the existing attack trigger, used here as a draft scoring criterion. |
| Warning and pass | Recover inside if the lane opens and keep playing. | Recovery can redirect or disrupt the pass. The review keeps the earlier missed read and reports the puck outcome separately. |

The existing draft coverage-time ratio and minimum observation window still apply when Red has no slot look. The approved Original and Marked timing and geometry are unchanged by this rule. The scenario uses simulation coordinates. Coach review should validate the visible spacing and timing on the rink.

## Source basis

- Johnston and Walter cover role assignments, system tradeoffs and transition cues. Chapter 10 covers defensive-zone coverage and Chapter 1 covers breakouts. [Human Kinetics contents](https://us.humankinetics.com/products/hockey-plays-and-strategies-2nd-edition) · [Public backcheck excerpt](https://us.humankinetics.com/blogs/excerpt/hound-the-puck)
- USA Hockey shows how to build pressure, support and net-front play into five-player coverage and use live games to teach decisions. [12U–18U D-Zone Build-Up](https://www.usahockey.com/news_article/show/703462-12u-18u-d-zone-build-up) · [Small-area games manual](https://cdn3.sportngin.com/attachments/document/0065/0924/Teaching-Concepts-Through-SAG.pdf)

- Denis Savard illustrates net-front ownership and communication before a partner releases. His system provides a comparison for the team’s review of each handoff. [Protect the House](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-systems/defensive-zone-coverage-protect-house)
